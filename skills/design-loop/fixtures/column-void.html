Why agents need experiments

Long-horizon agents drift when nothing checks their intermediate claims. The talk walks through a harness that turns each claim into an experiment with a recorded outcome, shows how the outcomes feed the next plan, and measures how often the plan changes once the evidence arrives. Most of the gain comes from refusing to carry an unverified claim forward, not from a larger model.
